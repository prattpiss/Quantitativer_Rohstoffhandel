Cohen's d: Effektstaerke. Median-Schaetzer gilt fuer symmetrische Verteilung (Fat Tails -> Median < Mean).
$$d=\frac{{\bar{{CAR}}}}{{\sigma_{{CAR}}}} \quad \text{{Median CAR}} \approx 0{,}94\cdot\bar{{CAR}}\text{{ (symmetr.)}}$$
